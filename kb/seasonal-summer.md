## Setpoint
Setting the thermostat to 60 does not cool the house faster. The system cools at the same rate until it reaches the setpoint, and 60 just makes it run longer. Keep west blinds shut in the afternoon.

## Humidity
If the indoor humidity stays high while the temperature drops, tell us. That is often an oversized system or a fan stuck in the On position. Fan Auto is the right everyday setting.

## Filters in July
A clean filter matters more in July than in March. Check a 1-inch filter monthly in midsummer.
