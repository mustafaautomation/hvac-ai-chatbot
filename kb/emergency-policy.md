## Same-day heating and cooling
No cooling on an Austin afternoon, or no heat on a freeze night, can be booked as a priority visit. Labor is priced higher than a flexible appointment, and a priority dispatch fee is added. We stop taking new same-day windows after 4 p.m. Saturday afternoon and Sunday are not regular windows.

## After the fire department
If you smell gas or a carbon monoxide alarm is sounding, leave and call 911. This page is the policy, not a live emergency. After the fire department has cleared the house, we can schedule the repair. The chat will not troubleshoot an active gas smell or a sounding carbon monoxide alarm.

## Fees
After-hours work that is already on the board still shows on the dispatch calendar. A late cancel inside four hours of the window notes a $79 sample fee in this demo.
