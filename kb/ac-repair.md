## What the visit includes
A technician diagnoses the indoor air handler and the outdoor condenser. Common Austin failures are capacitors and contactors that fail in the heat, dirty filters that freeze the coil, and low refrigerant from a leak. A leak is not a top-off we pretend will last.

## What we will not walk you through
We will not tell you to open the electrical compartment, handle refrigerant, or jump a contactor. Homeowner checks stop at the thermostat, the filter, and one breaker reset. If the breaker trips again, leave it off.

## After the diagnosis
You get a written repair price before we replace parts. If the equipment is older than 12 years, we will also tell you what a replacement would look like.
