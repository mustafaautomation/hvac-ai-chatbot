## What is in the price
A gas furnace install for a typical Austin home includes 80% to 96% AFUE equipment, labor, the City of Austin permit, flue work, and a thermostat. Homes replacing a system older than 15 years often need duct transitions or a new plenum.

## Sizing
We size the furnace to the square footage and the duct system, not to the biggest unit on the truck. An oversized furnace short-cycles and leaves rooms clammy.

## After install
Register the 10-year parts warranty within 60 days. Our labor warranty on the install is 2 years.
