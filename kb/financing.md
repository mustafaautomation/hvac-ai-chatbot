## Sample terms
Sample financing is 0% for 12 months on jobs over $3,000 through Hill Country Finance, a fictional lender. Credit approval would be required at a real company. This chat does not pull credit, store a social security number, or send you to a lender.

## Where it shows up
The quote card shows the financing note only when the top of the range is at least $3,000. That is typical for a furnace installation, a heat pump, or a new air conditioner. Repairs under that line are paid at the visit.

## Comfort Club
The maintenance plan is not financed. It is a small annual fee, paid when you join.
