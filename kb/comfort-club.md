## What Comfort Club includes
Comfort Club is Northwind Comfort's maintenance plan for one heating or cooling system. The annual price is $189-$228. It includes two seasonal tune-ups, a waived diagnostic fee, 15% off repairs, and priority scheduling when the board is full.

## What a tune-up covers
A tune-up measures supply and return temperatures, checks the capacitor and contactor, clears the condensate drain, and confirms the filter size. It is not an insurance policy and it does not include parts.

## Second systems
The price is for one system. A second system is the same price again. Homes over 3,000 sq ft in Austin often have two systems. A tune-up on equipment older than 12 years often turns into a repair quote, and we say so before we sell parts.
