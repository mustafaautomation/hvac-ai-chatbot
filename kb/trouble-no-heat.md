## Simple checks
Set the thermostat to Heat and the fan to Auto. A collapsed filter can shut a furnace down on a limit switch. Replace a filter you cannot see through, then wait 10 minutes.

## What usually fails
Igniters and flame sensors are the common repairs on gas furnaces in Austin. We do not sell a heat-exchanger patch. A cracked heat exchanger is a replacement conversation.

## Stop
Do not keep resetting a furnace that smells hot or that you suspect is leaking exhaust. Any smell of gas is an emergency: leave and call 911. This note is not a script for a gas smell.
