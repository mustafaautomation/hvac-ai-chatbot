title: Troubleshooting when the AC is not cooling
topics: not cooling, warm air, filter, breaker
summary: Set the thermostat to Cool and the fan to Auto, replace a packed filter, and confirm the outdoor fan is spinning.
---

## Thermostat
Set the mode to Cool, not Fan. Set the fan to Auto. Put the setpoint a few degrees below the room temperature and wait 10 minutes. A schedule can undo the change as soon as you walk away.

## Filter and outdoor unit
Replace a filter you cannot see light through. The outdoor fan should be spinning. The large copper line should feel cold, not frosty. Reset a tripped breaker once. If it trips again, leave it off.

## Stop
Ice, a burning smell, or a breaker that will not hold are technician jobs. Do not open the condenser cabinet.
