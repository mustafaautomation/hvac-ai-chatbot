## What is included
We clean supply and return trunks, the registers, and the blower compartment. Homes up to 1,500 sq ft start in the $299-$399 range, then about $70-$90 for each extra 500 sq ft. Older ducts may get a sanitizing treatment if they need it.

## What it will not fix
Duct cleaning does not repair a frozen coil, a bad compressor, or a room that is hot because a damper is closed. If the system is not cooling, book a repair. Cleaning is the right job when the equipment works and the vents are the complaint.

## Access
We need a clear path to the indoor unit and the attic hatch if the ducts are in the attic. Kennel pets that do not like vacuums.
