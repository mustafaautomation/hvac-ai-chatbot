title: Troubleshooting a thermostat
topics: thermostat, blank screen, batteries, schedule
summary: A blank thermostat is often batteries or an open blower door. Turn the schedule off before you decide the equipment is dead.
---

## Blank screen
Replace the batteries if the thermostat takes them. Close the blower door until the door switch clicks. Many furnaces will not run with that door ajar, and the thermostat then looks dead.

## One-degree test
Turn the schedule off for an hour and set Heat or Cool yourself. Change the setpoint by at least 3 degrees. You should hear a click, then the equipment within a couple of minutes. No click usually means the thermostat or the control board.

## Stop
If the thermostat or the wire nuts are hot, discolored, or smell like hot plastic, turn the breaker off. Do not keep power on a scorched stat.
