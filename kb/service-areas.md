## Core Austin
Downtown 78701, East Cesar Chavez 78702, Tarrytown 78703, and South Congress 78704 are core Austin with no trip fee. Hyde Park, Mueller, Circle C, Oak Hill, and the other core 787 ZIPs on the board are the same.

## Extended suburbs
Cedar Park 78613, Pflugerville 78660, Round Rock 78664, and Leander 78641 carry a $49 trip fee. Lakeway, Bee Cave, and Dripping Springs are also $49. Georgetown 78628 and Bastrop 78602 carry a $79 trip fee.

## Outside
We do not run trucks to Dallas 75201, Houston, or San Antonio. Ask with the ZIP and the desk checks the board instead of guessing.
