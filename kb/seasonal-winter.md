## Cedar pollen
Austin cedar season packs a 1-inch filter in a few weeks. Check the filter monthly from December through February. A 4-inch filter can go longer. Point the arrow on the filter toward the equipment.

## The rare freeze
Cover hose bibs, drip indoor faucets, and open cabinet doors under sinks. A heat pump may steam during defrost. That is not smoke.

## Heat
If the furnace is needed only a handful of nights, a heat pump is often the simpler system. We can price either one.
