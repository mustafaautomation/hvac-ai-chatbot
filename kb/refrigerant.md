## Leak first
If the system is low on refrigerant, the refrigerant went somewhere. We look for the leak before we add refrigerant. A top-off without a leak search is how the same call comes back in August.

## What we handle
We work on R-410A and newer R-32 residential systems. We do not ask a homeowner to buy refrigerant or to hook up gauges. Gauges and recovery stay with the technician.

## Frozen coil
A low charge freezes the coil the same way a dirty filter does. Melt the ice, then book the leak search if it returns.
