## New equipment
A new furnace, air conditioner, or heat pump includes a 10-year parts warranty if you register it with the manufacturer within 60 days. Labor from Northwind Comfort is 2 years. Comfort Club members can extend labor coverage to 5 years on that install. We hand you the registration form. Sending it in is the homeowner's step.

## Repairs
Repair parts and our labor are warranted for 1 year. Refrigerant added during a leak repair is not warranted if the leak is somewhere else and you declined the leak search.

## What is not covered
Filters, thermostat batteries, storm damage, and equipment we did not install or maintain are not covered. A warranty visit still needs an appointment on the board.
