## Diagnostic fee
The diagnostic visit is $89-$129. It is waived when you approve the repair, and it is waived for Comfort Club members. We do not quote a sealed-system repair over the phone. The chat quote tool is the sample range this demo stands behind.

## How ranges work
Same-day work adds a premium on labor. Flexible timing takes a little off labor. Parts climb with the age of the system. Installs itemize equipment, labor, permit, and thermostat. Duct cleaning is priced from square footage. These are sample ranges for a fictional company, not a bid.

## Replacement conversation
If a system is over 12 years old and the repair would pass half the price of a new one, we say so before we start.
