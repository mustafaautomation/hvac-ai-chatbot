## The half-price rule
If the equipment is over 12 years old and the repair would pass half the price of a new system, we pause and talk about replacement. A compressor on a 14-year-old air conditioner is the usual example.

## What replacement includes
A replacement quote itemizes equipment, labor, the permit, and a thermostat. Heat pump quotes also include the electrical disconnect. We do not bury a new line set inside a single number when the old one is undersized.

## Comfort Club after
A new system can go on Comfort Club at the install so the first two tune-ups are already scheduled.
