title: Troubleshooting a frozen coil
topics: frozen, ice, coil, frost, copper line
summary: Ice on the coil or the suction line means turn cooling off and let it melt. Do not chip the ice.
---

## Melt the ice first
If you see ice on the indoor coil or the large copper line outside, set the thermostat to Off. Leave the fan On if the blower still runs. Let the ice melt for one to three hours. Put towels down if the air handler is in a closet.

## Do not chip the ice
Do not scrape the coil and do not pour hot water on it. Bent fins and a cracked coil cost more than the visit. A clogged filter is the usual cause. After the melt, install a clean filter.

## When it comes back
If the ice returns the same day, if water comes through the ceiling, or if the breaker trips, book a repair. Low refrigerant and a failed blower freeze a coil the same way a dirty filter does. Running a frozen system can kill the compressor.
