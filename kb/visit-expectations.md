## The window
Arrival windows are two hours, America/Chicago. Weekdays run 8 a.m. to 6 p.m. Saturday runs 8 a.m. to 2 p.m. Sunday is closed except visits already on the board. We text a reminder in a real company. This demo does not send a text.

## At the house
Someone 18 or older should be home. Clear a path to the indoor unit and the outdoor condenser. Secure pets. The technician diagnoses first, then gives a price before replacing parts.

## Confirmation
Your confirmation code looks like NW-1842. You can look it up, move it, or cancel it in the chat. Cancel more than four hours ahead and there is no sample fee.
