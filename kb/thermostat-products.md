## Before you buy one
A blank screen is often two batteries or a blower door that is not closed. Try those before you buy a new thermostat. If there is no click when you change the setpoint by 3 degrees, the stat or the control board is the likely fault.

## The visit
A thermostat service includes the diagnostic, the thermostat, and labor. Smart thermostats often need a C-wire. If the air handler does not have one, we say so before we mount the new stat.

## Safety
If the old thermostat is hot or discolored, turn the breaker off and wait for the technician. Do not keep power on a scorched stat.
