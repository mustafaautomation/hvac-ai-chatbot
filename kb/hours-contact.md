## Shop
Northwind Comfort Heating & Air is a fictional shop at 1840 E 6th Street, Austin, TX 78702. The phone (512) 555-0198 is not a real dispatch line. Do not call it for a real emergency.

## Hours
Monday through Friday, 7:30 a.m. to 6:00 p.m. Saturday, 8:00 a.m. to 2:00 p.m. Sunday closed, except visits already on the board. The first arrival window is 8:00 a.m.

## Office versus truck
The office opens at 7:30 a.m. The first truck window is 8:00 a.m. Asking for a person in the chat opens a handoff on the dispatch board. Nobody is actually dialed in this demo.
