## Humidity first
In Austin the usual indoor-air complaint in summer is humidity, not a missing accessory. Fan Auto, a right-sized system, and a clean filter do more than a gadget. If the house is cool but clammy, tell us the thermostat fan setting.

## Add-ons
A dehumidifier or a UV light can be added after the system is cooling correctly. We will not sell one as the fix for a frozen coil or a dead capacitor.

## Ducts
If the equipment is fine and the complaint is dust at the registers after a remodel, duct cleaning is the matching job.
