## Why Austin homes use them
A heat pump moves heat instead of burning gas. In a mild winter that is usually cheaper to run, and it can replace both the air conditioner and the furnace. On the few freezing nights the outdoor unit will frost and run a defrost cycle.

## Steam is normal
Steam coming off the outdoor unit on a cold morning is normal defrost, not smoke. If auxiliary heat runs for days, the outdoor unit may be iced past a normal defrost and needs a visit.

## The quote
The quote covers equipment sized to the home, installation labor, the electrical disconnect, and the permit. Systems older than 15 years often need a new line set.
