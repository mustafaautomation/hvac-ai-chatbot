## How to tell
Hold the filter up to a lamp. If you cannot see light through it, replace it. Write the date on the frame. A packed filter is the most common reason a coil freezes in Austin.

## MERV
Most Northwind homes do well with MERV 8 to MERV 11. A MERV 13 filter in a 1-inch slot can starve an older blower. If the manufacturer calls for a 1-inch filter, do not fold a thicker one into the rack.
