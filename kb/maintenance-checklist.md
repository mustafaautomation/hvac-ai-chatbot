---

## Monthly
Change or check the filter. Keep two feet of clearance around the outdoor condenser. Straighten debris off the top. Do not spray the fins with a pressure washer.

## Each season
Pour a cup of vinegar into the condensate drain access if you can reach it without opening an electrical panel. Make sure the drain is not dripping into a pan that is already full.

## Never
Do not open the condenser cabinet, do not push in a contactor, and do not add refrigerant. Those are technician jobs.
