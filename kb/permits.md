## What we pull
Furnace, air conditioner, and heat pump replacements include the City of Austin permit in the quoted range. We schedule the inspection. A homeowner does not pull the mechanical permit for equipment we install.

## What can change the price
If the inspector requires a new disconnect, a flue liner, or a platform in the attic, that shows up as its own line before we do the work. We do not hide it inside the original equipment number after the fact.

## Repairs
A repair that does not replace the condenser or the furnace usually does not need a permit. We will say so on the visit.
